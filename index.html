📖 Family & Gpa User Instructions ▼
Tap for instructions on groceries, claiming dishes, voice notes, live sync & Gpa's admin controls.

1. Setting Up Your Device, Passcode & Name:

  • The very first time you open the link on a phone, tablet, or computer, select your name and enter the family passcode (Feast2026) to unlock the live cloud vault.
  • Once unlocked, your device remembers the passcode automatically! Sharing an iPad or iPhone? Tap Switch next to your name in the top banner to switch family members without re-entering the passcode.

2. 🛒 Recipe Grocery Checklist, Claiming, Editing & Recovery:

  • Claim an Item Ahead of Time (🙋 Claim): Tap 🙋 Claim next to any ingredient or dish to assign your name to picking it up (e.g., 📌 Bruce) before you head to the store.
  • Check Off Purchased Items: Tap any checkbox once bought—it crosses off the ingredient, updates the progress bar, and stamps your name next to it (e.g., ✓ Bruce).
  • Add & Edit Items: Pick a recipe category in the Add Ingredient or Item box to add new items, or tap ✏️ to rename/change quantities.
  • Undo Deleted Items (Trash Vault): Tapping ✕ moves an item into the 🗑️ Trash vault at the bottom of the grocery drawer where you can tap ♻️ Restore anytime.

3. 🍗 Culinary Teams & 🎤 Team Voice Notes: Tap any team card to see who is assigned to each dish, or tap 🎤 Leave Team Voice Note inside a team drawer to record an audio update for your team.

4. 🎨 Master Graffiti Board & Emoji Reactions:

  • Choose a mood tag (🟢 Excitement, 🔴 Logistics, or 🟡 Quirky), type a message, attach a photo/video/audio file, or tap 🎤 Leave Voice Note right next to Post to Fridge.
  • Tap any emoji pill (❤️ 🦃 🔥 😂 🙌) under posts in the Live Streams feed to react, or tap 💬 Reply to leave a comment directly on a family member's post!

5. 🗄️ Personal Streams & Media Scrapbook Downloads: Tap 📂 Open My Personal Stream & Vault to filter your contributions, restore deleted posts, or download a standalone playable .html scrapbook containing all of your voice notes, photos, and videos.

6. 🌐 Automatic Live Cloud Sync:

  • All updates—including grocery check-offs, dish claims, voice notes, photos, and emoji reactions—sync automatically in real time via Firebase across every family device!
⚡ Gpa Administrative Protocol:
  • Unlock Command Center: Scroll to the very bottom and triple-click the footer text (🐾 Celebration Turkey-Curry HQ • 2026 (v15)), then enter passcode Turk2026.
  • Reassign Teams & Dishes: Select a family member and target team, then click Execute Team Reassignment—both team rosters and specific dish assignments update live across all devices.
  • Cloud Database Controls: View or update the live Firebase Database URL, force-push local state to the cloud, or download Master Family Archives (.html and .txt).
